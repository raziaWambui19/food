Ugali

ugali is a popular traditional african food,especially common in KENYA,TANZANIA and UGANDA.
Its nomally eaten with

  • sukuma wiki
  • nyama(meat stew)
  • fish
  • beans
  • vegetable stew

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup maize flour(cornmeal)
  • (optional) a pinch of salt

Nyama choma

Nyama choma is a popular East African dish.especially famous in KENYA and TANZANIA.The name literally means "roasted meat" in swahili. Its nomally eaten with

  • Ugali
  • Kachumbari(tomato + onion salad)
  • sukuma wiki
  • sometimes chapati or fries

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 kg goat meat(or beef)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper(optional)
  • 1 tsp paprika(optional)
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice (optional, for tenderness)
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil (optional)
